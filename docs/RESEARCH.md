## Accuracy Limits and Decisions

- Measurements are product dimensions, not CAD-derived geometry. Photo perspective and crop produce an apparent front ratio different from 113:48. The photograph is not stretched to conceal this difference.
- 133 g is the standard published figure, not bare chassis mass; removable battery variants may affect weight.
- The third-party manual transcription is a later edition covering the original model family, not a verified launch-year scan.
- Sources call the navigation inputs scroll keys. A mechanically single rocker was not independently verified.
- No reliable engineering drawing was found for exact screen-window dimensions, key spacing, corner radii, polymer grade, or shell thickness. Those values are not invented.
- The photograph's blue shell, softened key surfaces, white wordmark, vertical earpiece, and regional keypad legends are retained. Its original LCD content is replaced, and the active screen area is expanded slightly for reading comfort.
- The logical LCD resolution is exact; the custom font, menu content, page count, session-progress battery, and screen state are portfolio adaptations, not a ROM emulator.
- Browser controls use enlarged hit regions. On short screens the page scrolls normally to preserve access to the whole handset.
- No startup delay, Snake game, copied startup animation, contact dialing, or arbitrary nostalgic props were added. These would not improve access to the supplied professional content.
